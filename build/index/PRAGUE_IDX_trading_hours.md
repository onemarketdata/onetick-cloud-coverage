# Prague Stock Exchange - Indices Trading Hours

Index values from the Prague Stock Exchange, with history from February 2018. The main index is the PX, the benchmark of the most liquid Czech blue chips, together with its total return version (PX-TR).

Trading Hours are divided into multiple sessions
