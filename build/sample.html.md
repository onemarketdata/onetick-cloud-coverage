# Sample Data

Sample Data is available for all subscribers and those institutions undertaking a 30 day trial.
It provides access to historic data from 1st January 2024 to 31st March 2024.

It covers:

* [AU_COMP_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/AU_COMP_SAMPLE.html.md) - Australian Consolidated Equities Sample
* [CA_COMP_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/CA_COMP_SAMPLE.html.md) - Canadian Consolidated Market Sample
* [CME_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/CME_SAMPLE.html.md) - CME Group (PCAP) Sample
* [EU_COMP_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/EU_COMP_SAMPLE.html.md) - European Consolidated Equities Sample
* [EUREX_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/EUREX_SAMPLE.html.md) - Eurex Sample
* [GLOBAL_FX_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/GLOBAL_FX_SAMPLE.html.md) - Global FX (Spots, Forwards, NDFs) Sample
* [ICE_EU_COM_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/ICE_EU_COM_SAMPLE.html.md) - ICE Europe Commodities Sample
* [ICE_US_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/ICE_US_SAMPLE.html.md) - ICE US Sample
* [JP_COMP_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/JP_COMP_SAMPLE.html.md) - Japan Consolidated Equities Sample
* [LSE_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/LSE_SAMPLE.html.md) - London Stock Exchange - UK Market Sample
* [TDI_FUT_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/TDI_FUT_SAMPLE.html.md) - Global Curated Futures Sample
* [US_COMP_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/US_COMP_SAMPLE.html.md) - US Consolidated Equities (exc. OTC) Sample
* [US_COMP_REPLAY](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/US_COMP_REPLAY.html.md) - US Consolidated Equity Replay for Intraday / CEP
* [US_OPTIONS_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/US_OPTIONS_SAMPLE.html.md) - US Consolidated Options (OPRA) Sample
* [US_OPTIONS_EOD_SAMPLE](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample/US_OPTIONS_EOD_SAMPLE.html.md) - US Consolidated Options (OPRA) EOD - OPRA Daily Volume, OI and Greeks Sample
