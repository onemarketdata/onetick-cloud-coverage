# Coverage

On Demand Market Data access is available for Global Equities, Futures & Options.

Data Assets are divided into:

* [Sample Data](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/sample.html.md) - Covering the first 3 months of 2024
* [Consolidated Fragmented Liquidity](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated.html.md) - Combining Fragmented Liquidity from Multiple Markets
* [Exchanges - Cash](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/cash.html.md) - Global List of Equity Exchanges
* [Exchanges - Derivatives](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/derivatives.html.md) - Global List of Futures & Options Exchanges
* [Indices](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/indices.html.md) - Global List of Index Providers
* [FX & Metals](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/fx.html.md) - FX and Spot Metals
* [Interest rates](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/interest_rates.html.md) - Daily Interest Rates
* [Indices](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/indices.html.md) - Global List of Index Providers
* [OQD - One Quant Data](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/oqd.html.md) - Security Master, Corporate Actions, Closing Prices, US ETF Constituents
* [Reference](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/ref.html.md) - Reference Databases

Data includes:

* Tick Data - L1 Trade & Quote Data through to L3 Book Depth
* Bar Data - 1 Minute Calculuated Trade & Quote Bars
* Daily Data - Daily OHLC Data, and for Derivatives Settlement and Open Interest
