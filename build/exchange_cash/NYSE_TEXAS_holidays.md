NYSE Texas is a fully electronic equities exchange headquartered in Dallas, part of the New York Stock Exchange group owned by Intercontinental Exchange (ICE). Launched in 2025 by reincorporating and rebranding NYSE Chicago, it provides a dedicated listing and trading venue for companies in Texas and the Southwest.
