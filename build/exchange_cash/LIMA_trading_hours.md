# Lima Stock Exchange Trading Hours

The Lima Stock Exchange is the official stock exchange of Peru where securities like stocks, bonds and derivatives are traded.

Trading Hours are divided into multiple sessions

#### Lima Stock Exchange - Trading Hours

| Session Name   | Time Zone    | Start Time   | End Time   |
|----------------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| PRE_MARKET     | America/Lima | 09:00        | 09:30      |
| MARKET         | America/Lima | 09:30        | 16:00      |
| POST_MARKET    | America/Lima | 16:00        | 16:10      |
