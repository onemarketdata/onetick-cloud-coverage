# Vienna Stock Exchange Trading Hours

Trades, quotes, bars and book depth, with real-time data, from the Vienna Stock Exchange (Wiener Börse), Austria’s stock exchange, with history from June 2012, covering its prime, standard, direct and global market segments. Covered stocks include ATX constituents, and Wiener Börse also calculates Central and Eastern European indices such as CECE; index values are in VIENNA_IDX.  A consolidated tape for all European equity markets is provided in the EU_COMP dataset.

Trading Hours are divided into multiple sessions
