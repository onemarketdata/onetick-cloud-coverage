# NYSE American Equities Trading Hours

Trades, Quotes and Book depth from NYSE American (formerly the American Stock Exchange, AMEX), with history from May 2025. NYSE American is a fully electronic US equities exchange owned by NYSE’s parent company, Intercontinental Exchange. It focuses on listing small- and mid-cap companies and also trades other NMS securities. Full L1 history for NYSE American from the Consolidated Tape is available in the US_COMP dataset.
