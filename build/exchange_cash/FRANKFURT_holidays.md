# Frankfurt Stock Exchange Market Holidays

Trades, quotes and bars from Börse Frankfurt, the Frankfurt Stock Exchange’s specialist-based market operated by Deutsche Börse, with history from December 2018. It is aimed at retail investors and offers a very large range of international shares, bonds, ETFs and funds with specialist quotes; for Germany’s main electronic market use XETRA. Covered stocks include DAX constituents.  A consolidated tape for all European equity markets is provided in the EU_COMP dataset.
