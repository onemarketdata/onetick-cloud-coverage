# Nextrade ATS Trading Hours

Nextrade (NXT) is South Korea’s first licensed Alternative Trading System (ATS), operating alongside the traditional Korea Exchange.

Trading Hours are divided into multiple sessions

#### Nextrade ATS - Trading Hours

| Session Name   | Time Zone   | Start Time   | End Time   |
|----------------|-------------|--------------|------------|
| PRE_MARKET     | Asia/Seoul  | 08:00        | 08:50      |
| MARKET         | Asia/Seoul  | 09:00        | 15:20      |
| POST_MARKET    | Asia/Seoul  | 15:30        | 20:00      |
