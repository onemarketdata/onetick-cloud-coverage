# Consolidated Fragmented Liquidity

Fragmented liquidity from multiple markets are combined into a single databased per region.
Additionally includes curated datasets, where key products are combined from multiple markets

It covers:

* [AU_COMP](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated/AU_COMP.html.md) - Australian Consolidated Equities
* [CA_COMP](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated/CA_COMP.html.md) - Canadian Consolidated Equities (all Trades, Quotes, NBBO)
* [EU_COMP](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated/EU_COMP.html.md) - European Consolidated Equities
* [IN_COMP](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated/IN_COMP.html.md) - India Consolidated Equities
* [JP_COMP](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated/JP_COMP.html.md) - Japan Consolidated Equities
* [KR_COMP](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated/KR_COMP.html.md) - Korea Consolidated Equities
* [MX_COMP](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated/MX_COMP.html.md) - Mexico Consolidated Equities
* [TDI_FUT](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated/TDI_FUT.html.md) - Global Futures
* [TDI_IDX](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated/TDI_IDX.html.md) - Global Indices
* [TW_COMP](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated/TW_COMP.html.md) - Taiwan Consolidated Equities
* [US_COMP](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated/US_COMP.html.md) - US Consolidated Equities (exc. OTC)
* [US_SHORT_INT](https://cloudcoverage.docs.sol.onetick.com/consolidated/US_SHORT_INT.html.md) - FINRA - US Equities Short Interest / Short Volume
