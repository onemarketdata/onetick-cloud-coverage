# US Consolidated Options (OPRA) Trading Hours

Consolidated trades and quotes for all US-listed equity, ETF and index options from the Options Price Reporting Authority (OPRA), covering all US options exchanges, with history from January 2021. OPRA is one of the largest market data feeds in the world, covering options on individual stocks, ETFs such as SPY and QQQ, and indices such as the S&P 500 (SPX).

Trading Hours are divided into multiple sessions
