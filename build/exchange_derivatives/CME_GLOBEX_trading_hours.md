# CME Globex Futures Trading Hours

Futures and spreads with Trades, Quotes, bars, book depth and real-time data for contracts listed on the Chicago Mercantile Exchange and traded on CME Globex, with history from November 2009. Key contracts include E-mini and Micro E-mini S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 futures, SOFR futures, FX futures such as euro and yen, and livestock futures. For all CME Group exchanges with MBO book depth, use CME.
