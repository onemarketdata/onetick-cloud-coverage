# Eurex Sample Market Holidays

A sample of the Eurex database covering a subset of symbols between January 2, 2024 and March 31, 2024. Eurex is the derivatives exchange of Deutsche Boerse Group and Europe’s largest futures and options market. The sample includes trades, quotes and bars for futures, options and spreads on FDAX (DAX), FESX (EURO STOXX 50) and FTUK (FTSE 100), Eurex’s benchmark equity index contracts.
