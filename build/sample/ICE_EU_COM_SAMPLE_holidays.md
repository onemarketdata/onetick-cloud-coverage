# ICE Europe Commodities Sample Market Holidays

A sample of the ICE Futures Europe commodities database covering a subset of symbols between January 2, 2024 and March 31, 2024. ICE Futures Europe is the London-based exchange that hosts the Brent crude benchmark and the former Liffe soft commodity contracts. The sample includes trades, quotes, book depth and bars for futures, options and spreads on BRN (Brent Crude), WBS (WTI Crude), RC (Robusta Coffee) and W (White Sugar).
