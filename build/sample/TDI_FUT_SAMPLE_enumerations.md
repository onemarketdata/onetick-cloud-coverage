# Global Curated Futures Sample Enumerations

A sample of the TDI_FUT database, a curated list of more than 280 of the most liquid global futures contracts traded across 25+ markets, between January 2, 2024 and March 31, 2024. Contracts span equity indices, interest rates, government bonds, currencies, energy, metals and agriculture, and bars are included for cross-asset backtesting.
